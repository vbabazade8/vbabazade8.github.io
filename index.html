Kernl

A daily AI briefing on Azerbaijani business news for executives and investors, in English, Azerbaijani and Russian.

  1. Collect Scrapers for apa.az and axar.az plus 16 RSS feeds, every 4 hours
  2. Store 400–600 news a day in PostgreSQL (Neon)
  3. Summarize Gemini as a senior analyst, one digest per industry
  4. Deliver Website, email and Telegram, every morning
  • Turns hundreds of articles into a five-minute read for four industries: investment, banking, energy and real estate.
  • Every fact in a digest links to the original article, so readers can check it.
  • Runs on its own with GitHub Actions. Accounts with email codes, CSRF protection and rate limits are built in.

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Gemini API, GitHub Actions, Vercel

Baku apartment prices

A model that estimates the price of an apartment in Baku from its area, rooms, floor and district.

  1. Collect 58,675 listings from the bina.az GraphQL API
  2. Clean Duplicates and rentals removed, 44,382 listings left
  3. Train 4 models compared, gradient boosting tuned with Optuna
  4. Serve FastAPI and a simple form, in Docker
  • The average error went from about 120,000 to 37,500 AZN (R² 0.83) as the dataset grew from 1,400 to 44,000 listings.
  • Area and district matter most; the paid "VIP" listing flag barely matters at all.
  • Each step is a separate notebook, so the reasoning behind every change can be followed.

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Optuna, FastAPI, Docker
